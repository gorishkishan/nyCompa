Questions owners ask us.

Not on the list? Message us and we will answer in plain words.

I do not know much about websites or ads. Is that okay?

Yes, that is exactly who we work with. You tell us about your business and we handle the technical part. We explain every step in simple Hindi or English.

How much should I spend on ads?

It depends on your business and your area. We suggest a starting budget that suits you, and you decide. You can start small and increase it once you see enquiries coming in.

Will I own my website and ad accounts?

Yes. Everything is created in your name, so it always belongs to you.

How soon will I see results?

Ads can start bringing enquiries soon after they go live. Your website needs a little time to be designed and approved by you first. We tell you what to expect before we begin.

Do you work with very small businesses?

Yes. Many of our clients are single-shop owners and local service providers. We also work with larger companies and several branches.

How will I know it is working?

You get a simple monthly report with calls, enquiries and money spent. We also talk through it with you, so you are never left guessing.
